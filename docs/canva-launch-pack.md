# Canva Launch Pack Brief

## Brand

MacAltHub is a polished macOS catalog for finding verified direct-download alternatives to paid Mac utilities.

## Visual Style

- Clean native Mac interface, App Store-inspired.
- Backgrounds: soft blue-white, subtle glass, no heavy purple gradients.
- Product signal: catalog grid, app icons, verified download badges, comparison table.
- Typography: Apple system style, bold concise headlines, compact utility labels.

## Social Assets

1. Instagram post: "Stop hunting through Discord for Mac app downloads."
2. Twitter/X post: "55 verified alternatives to paid macOS utilities."
3. YouTube thumbnail: "Best free Mac utility alternatives."
4. Product launch card: catalog grid with direct download badge.

## Copy

- Headline: "Find the free Mac utility before you pay."
- Supporting: "Verified sources, direct downloads, no Discord gates."
- CTA: "Browse MacAltHub"

## Canva Generation Prompt

Create a polished launch social post for MacAltHub, a macOS-style hub for verified direct-download alternatives to paid Mac utility apps. Use a clean App Store-inspired interface mockup with app tiles, a blue direct download badge, and a subtle macOS glass surface. Include exact text: "Find the free Mac utility before you pay." Secondary text: "Verified sources. Direct downloads. No Discord gates." Use restrained blue, white, graphite, and green accents. Avoid neon, cartoon mascots, clutter, or generic SaaS gradients.
